Technical and research expertise

Landscape-scale spatial analysis. R, Python, Google Earth Engine, and ArcGIS Pro pipelines for processing remote sensing and other spatial data at landscape and statewide extents; statistical treatment of spatial autocorrelation and interpolation of discrete data sources.

Biophysical modeling. Large-scale modeling of biophysical processes, including wind velocity using NOAA’s High-Resolution Rapid Refresh atmospheric model with WindNinja, and streamflow using high-resolution digital elevation models and hydrologic models implemented in R.

Biological inventory and monitoring. Design and implementation of field data collection and processing for inventory and monitoring: mobile data platforms in Esri Field Maps and Survey123; rare and invasive plant mapping; meadow and rangeland monitoring design; herbarium voucher collection and curation; iNaturalist projects for crowdsourced data; camera trapping and AI-assisted identification (Addax AI, Wildlife Insights).

Data science and computing. High-performance computing clusters and parallel processing; analysis of biodiversity datasets of millions of records to produce diversity statistics, range maps, and species interaction networks; reproducible workflows in R and Python, version control with Git.

Genomics and bioinformatics. Target capture, RAD-seq, and whole-genome resequencing analysis in HPC environments; phylogenomic and population genomic inference, species delimitation, and hybridization analysis.
